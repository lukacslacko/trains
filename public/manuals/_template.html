§ 1 Principles

Every chapter opens with a lead paragraph in the voice of a calm colleague: what this chapter is for, and why you might read it.

Write it down so well that people want to read it.Drawing office inscription

1.1 Rules and notes

Rules are imperative and numbered. Cite them across books as Rule R 20.

Rule X 10The shape of a rule

State the rule in one or two sentences. Say what to do, not what not to do, where possible.

Every rule that could puzzle a newcomer gets a short explanation of its purpose. We would rather be understood than obeyed.

Notes carry practical detail: exceptions, measurements, cross-references.
