Six entries, in Book order. Occasion: the Coldwater extension brings distant signals, subsidiaries, whistle boards and the neutral section's boards to the line, and Book T to the shelf.

§ 7.3 Distant signals and subsidiaries New section

Why: two things joined the signal posts with the extension and the Works, the Signal Engineer and the driver must mean the same object by them.

A distant signal is the standard head reduced to two lamps, green above amber, on the standard Slate post, with an Ivory chevron plate behind the head: a V pointing down, 600 mm across and 400 mm deep, with an Ink rim 40 mm wide. The chevron says this signal warns, it does not stop you. The plate carries the home's name with the letter D added, in the standard plate of § 7.2: WD 1D is the distant for WD 1. A distant stands one warning distance before its home, 200 m where the approach is level or rising and 250 m where it falls at 10‰ or more, on the same side of the line as the home.

A subsidiary is a small Ink box, 300 × 200 mm, fixed beside the head of a main signal at the level of its lower lamp, with two White lamps 60 mm across set in a diagonal rising to the right. Lit, with the main at STOP, it is the SHUNT aspect for a shunting move; unlit, it is nothing. Only the signals that Book S names carry one; on this line, WD 4 and WD 7.

Fig. 7.3 shows WD 1D with its chevron plate and a main signal at STOP with its subsidiary lit, dimensioned in brass; a definition list gives both in one line each.

§ 7.5 Boards Figure changed · Wording changed

Why: three boards joined the four, for the crossing and the neutral section on the Coldwater section.

Four fixed boards are in use, drawn here so that the Works and Book S agree.

Fig. 7.4: Stop board, Limit of Shunt board, Speed board and Advance speed board. Definition list of the four.

Seven fixed boards are in use, drawn here so that the Works and Book S agree.

Fig. 7.5 gains the Whistle board, the Section board and the Resume board. Definition list adds: Whistle board. The speed board's disc, Ivory, 600 mm across with an Ink rim 40 mm wide, carrying the letter W in Ink, Barlow Condensed 700, 300 mm high. Section board. Ink square 600 mm on a side, no rim, with a White bar 400 × 60 mm lying across the line, level to the driver: shut off power. Resume board. Ink square 600 mm on a side, no rim, with a White bar 400 × 60 mm lying along the line, upright to the driver: power may be taken.

New closing paragraph: a whistle board stands 200 m before a crossing, facing the trains that will reach it, one for each direction; a section board 90 m before a neutral section and a resume board 90 m beyond it, in each direction, so that a driver reads bar-across, the gap, bar-along. The two bars are the same bar turned, as the switch indicator's is, and they are White because they too are lamps. All three stand on the right of the direction they face, at the standard 2.0 m to the board centre.
