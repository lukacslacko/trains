Amendment Slip S-12

Book S, eleventh edition (September 2026) → twelfth edition (September 2026). Issued 10 September 2026. Paste inside the back cover of an eleventh-edition copy, or read it here to bring yourself up to date.

The twelfth edition corrects one drawing and the figures around it. The eleventh edition gave Ashgrove Shed three roads of seventy metres each, measured from their ladder switches. The ladder is a stagger, each switch thirty metres beyond the last, so roads of equal length ended at three different places: only road 1 reached the building, and the cars, stabled from the buffer stops outwards, stood over the ladder rather than under the roof. The Engineer has lengthened the roads to a common far wall: road 1 is 150 m from AG T, road 2 120 m from AG U, road 3 90 m from its lead, and all three end at buffer stops at shed km 0.181, with the building over their far sixty-two metres and the cars inside it. The switches, the exit signals, their kilometres, the speed and the routes are as they were. No rule changes its number. The entries below follow the Book's order.

§ 1 Principles Paragraph added · Wording changed

Why: each edition opens with a note saying what it changed; the eleventh edition's note is put in the past, as the tenth's was before it.

The chapter opened with the note "The shed": "This eleventh edition opens Ashgrove Shed: three stabling roads off the headshunt, a ladder of switches and three ground signals at the road exits … No rule changes its number."

A new note "The shed roads" opens the chapter: "This twelfth edition corrects one drawing and the figures around it. The three roads of Ashgrove Shed were drawn seventy metres long each from their ladder switches, which, with the ladder's stagger, brought only the first of them to the building and left the cars standing over the switches. The roads now run to a common far wall, 150, 120 and 90 metres from their switches, all three ending at buffer stops at shed km 0.181, with the building over their far sixty-two metres and the cars stabled inside it, clear of the ladder (§ 6.8, Fig. 6.8). No signal, switch or route changes, and no rule changes its number." The eleventh's note now reads "The eleventh edition opened Ashgrove Shed … From that edition the masters give line clear before the road is set … No rule changed its number."

§ 6.8 Ashgrove Shed Wording changed · Figure redrawn · Table row amended

Why: the roads' lengths and the buffer stops' kilometres were those of the first drawing, which the Engineer has since corrected.

"The three shed roads are seventy metres long each and end at buffer stops; the shed building stands over their far forty metres, so that a car put to bed is under a roof and a car brought out is in the open by the time it reaches its signal." Fig. 6.8 was headed "THREE ROADS OF 70 M" and drew the three roads ending one behind another, with the building over the far end of road 1 only; its caption said "each with its buffer stop and its exit signal four metres inside the road". The table's last row: "Buffer stops · 0.100 · 0.131 · 0.161 · roads 1, 2, 3 · End of track; red lamp. The last forty metres of each road are inside the building."

"The three shed roads run from their ladder switches to a common far wall, road 1 for 150 m, road 2 for 120 m and road 3 for 90 m, and end there at buffer stops, all three at shed km 0.181; the shed building stands over their far sixty-two metres, and the cars stable inside it, from the buffer stops outwards, well clear of the ladder, so that a car put to bed is under a roof and a car brought out is in the open long before it reaches its signal." Fig. 6.8 is headed "ROADS OF 150, 120 AND 90 M TO ONE WALL", draws the three roads reaching one wall with their three buffer stops in a line and the building over the far ends of all three, labels the roads with their lengths, and its caption says "each running to the same far wall, where its buffer stop stands, and each with its exit signal four metres inside the road; the building stands over the far ends of all three". The table's last row: "Buffer stops · 0.181 (all three) · roads 1, 2, 3 · End of track at a common far wall; red lamp. Roads 1, 2 and 3 are 150, 120 and 90 m long from their switches; the last sixty-two metres of each are inside the building."

§ 11 Glossary Entry amended

Why: the entry carried the old length.

"Shed road: One of the three roads of the shed, seventy metres long, a queue: the car nearest the door leaves first …"

"Shed road: One of the three roads of the shed, each running to the same far wall (150, 120 and 90 m from the ladder), a queue: the car nearest the door leaves first …"
