Amendment Slip D-10

Book D, ninth edition (September 2026) → tenth edition (September 2026). Issued 10 September 2026. Paste inside the back cover of a ninth-edition copy, or read it here to bring yourself up to date.

A short slip about a longer shed. The ninth edition described the three roads of Ashgrove Shed as seventy metres each from their ladder switches. The ladder staggers the roads, so at that length only the first of them reached the building, and the cars stabled from the buffer stops outwards stood back at the switches, over the leads, in the way of every move. The Engineer has laid the roads on to a common far wall: road 1 is a hundred and fifty metres from AG T, road 2 a hundred and twenty from AG U, road 3 ninety from its lead, all three ending at buffer stops at shed km 0.181, with the building over their far sixty-two metres. The cars now stable inside the building, well clear of the ladder, and the two shunts of the day are a little longer for it. Nothing else about the shed changes: the switch AG S, the leads, the exit signals four metres inside each road, shunting speed and the level road are as they were. No rule changes its number or its meaning. Book P's sixth edition carries the plan.

§ 3.1 Booking on at the shed Sentence amended

The roads' length and the building's place over them.

Each road is seventy metres long and ends at a buffer stop, with the shed building over the far forty.

The three roads run to a common far wall: road 1 a hundred and fifty metres from AG T, road 2 a hundred and twenty from AG U, road 3 ninety from its own lead; each ends at a buffer stop at shed km 0.181, and the shed building stands over the far sixty-two metres of all three. The cars stable inside the building, from the buffer stops outwards, so that the door end of a car is a good ninety metres from the ladder.

§ 3.2 Out of the shed: the first shunt Sentence added

A car that stands inside the building starts its shunt a long way short of its exit signal; the ninth edition had it draw straight out.

Your car stands well inside the building, some eighty metres short of its exit signal, which is four metres inside the road: the first part of the shunt is a creep along the road in the half-light up to the signal, and only past it do the ladder and the daylight come.

§ 7.1 Into the shed Warning amended

The distance from the ladder to the buffer stops, and where the door is.

The buffer stops in the shed are seventy metres from the ladder and inside a building, and the last of the light goes at the door.

The buffer stops in the shed are a hundred and fifty, a hundred and twenty and ninety metres from the ladder, all inside the building, and the last of the light goes at the door, sixty-two metres before them.

§ 12 Glossary · Shed road Entry amended

The same figures in the glossary.
