Amendment Slip I-7

Book I, sixth edition (September 2026) → seventh edition (September 2026). Issued 9 September 2026. Paste inside the back cover of a sixth-edition copy, or read it here to bring yourself up to date.

Three entries, in Book order. Occasion: the Fernhollow branch opens with a kilometrage of its own, and its plates must say so.

§ 7.1 Station name boards Wording changed · Figure changed

Why: a fourth station joins the Railway, with the longest name yet, and colleagues are now named in the Boxes' messages.

… Boards stand on two Slate posts, the lower edge 1.4 m above the platform, and are placed at both ends of every platform and opposite the stop board.

Fig. 7.1: Station name board, Ashgrove.

… Boards stand on two Slate posts, the lower edge 1.4 m above the platform, and are placed at both ends of every platform and opposite the stop board. Every station on the Railway has the same board; only the name changes, and a longer name, Fernhollow at the head of the branch, takes a longer board at the same height rather than smaller letters. The names of the people who work the Railway appear in the Boxes' messages and in the Books, never on a board and never on stock.

Fig. 7.1: Station name boards, Ashgrove and Fernhollow: the same board, a longer name on a longer board. The Fernhollow board is drawn beside the Ashgrove one.

§ 7.6 Hectometre and kilometre posts Wording changed · Figure changed · List changed

Why: the branch has its own kilometrage from the junction switch WD J, and a position quoted to a Box must never be mistaken for the main line's.

… the green rim alone says that a full kilometre has been reached. Posts are a position reference and nothing more. …

Fig. 7.6: Hectometre post at 1.4 km and kilometre post at 2.0 km. Definition list: Hectometre post; Kilometre post.

… the green rim alone says that a full kilometre has been reached. The Fernhollow branch has a kilometrage of its own, counted from the junction switch WD J, and every plate on it carries the letter F before the figure, in the same face and at the same height as the figures and separated from them by a figure space: F 0.1, F 2.0. The letter is the whole difference; a position quoted to a Box as "F 1.7" can never be taken for the main line's 1.7. Posts are a position reference and nothing more. …

Fig. 7.6 gains a third plate, the branch's hectometre plate reading F 0.1, drawn at the hectometre plate's size with the letter and the figure space called out. Definition list adds: Branch plates. The same two plates on the Fernhollow branch, each figure preceded by the letter F in the same face and height, separated by a figure space (F 0.1, F 2.0). The branch's kilometrage counts from the junction switch WD J.
