Amendment Slip I-5

Book I, fourth edition (September 2026) → fifth edition (September 2026). Issued 9 September 2026. Paste inside the back cover of a fourth-edition copy, or read it here to bring yourself up to date.

Two entries, in Book order. Occasion: the kilometre plate is redrawn so that it reads like its neighbours.

§ 7.5 Hectometre and kilometre posts Wording changed · Figure changed

Why: the first drawing set the kilometre in green figures under the word KM, and the figure merged with the rim; a plate that reads like its neighbours is read at a glance, so the kilometre plate now carries the kilometre with its zero in the same Ink figures, and only its green rim marks the full kilometre.

At every full kilometre the plate is a kilometre post instead: Ivory, 360 × 240 mm, with a Meridian Green border 12 mm wide, the word KM in green 40 mm high, and the kilometre alone beneath it in green figures 150 mm high, so that the post at 2 km reads 2.

Fig. 7.5: the kilometre plate with KM above a green figure 2; callouts 40 (the word KM) and 150 (the figure). Caption: "Hectometre post at 1.4 km and kilometre post at 2 km."

Definition list: Kilometre post. Ivory plate with a Meridian Green border, the word KM small above the kilometre in green figures. One at every full kilometre, in place of the hectometre post.

At every full kilometre the plate is a kilometre post instead: the same plate on a larger scale, Ivory, 360 × 240 mm, with a Meridian Green border 12 mm wide in place of the Slate one, and the kilometre with its zero in the same Ink figures, 150 mm high, so that the post at 2 km reads 2.0. It carries no other word or mark; a plate that reads like its neighbours is read at a glance, and the green rim alone says that a full kilometre has been reached.

Fig. 7.5: the kilometre plate reading 2.0 in Ink figures within the green rim; the callout 40 is withdrawn, 150 remains for the figures, 360 × 240 and BORDER 12 unchanged. Caption: "Hectometre post at 1.4 km and kilometre post at 2.0 km."

Definition list: Kilometre post. The hectometre plate on a larger scale, with a Meridian Green border, reading the kilometre with its zero in Ink figures (2.0). One at every full kilometre, in place of the hectometre post.
