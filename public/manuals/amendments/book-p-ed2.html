Amendment Slip P-2

Book P, first edition (September 2026) → second edition (September 2026). Issued 9 September 2026. Paste inside the back cover of a first-edition copy, or read it here to bring yourself up to date.

The second edition changes one sign. The kilometre post no longer carries the word KM over a green numeral: it is now the hectometre plate on a larger scale, reading the kilometre with its zero (1.0, 2.0, 3.0) in the same black figures, told apart only by its green rim. A driver reads every post the same way, and the full kilometres still announce themselves. No rule was renumbered.

§ 2 The line Wording changed

Why: the sentence on the posts now says what each plate reads, so that the kilometre post's new lettering is in this Book as well as in Book S.

The ground is marked every 100 m by a hectometre post and every full kilometre by a kilometre post, standing on the left of the line in the Down direction and moved outward past track 2 where a loop exists.

The ground is marked every 100 m by a hectometre post, an ivory plate reading the kilometre, a point and the hectometre digit (1.4), and every full kilometre by a kilometre post, which is the same plate on a larger scale, reading the kilometre with its zero (1.0, 2.0, 3.0) in the same black figures and distinguished only by a green rim. Both stand on the left of the line in the Down direction and are moved outward past track 2 where a loop exists.
