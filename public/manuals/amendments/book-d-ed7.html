Amendment Slip D-7

Book D, sixth edition (September 2026) → seventh edition (September 2026). Issued 9 September 2026. Paste inside the back cover of a sixth-edition copy, or read it here to bring yourself up to date.

A small edition. The Signal Engineer has settled where a subsidiary lives: not out to the side of the main head, where its bracket would break the outline of the post, but on the post directly under the head, so that head and subsidiary read as one signal. Book D says so wherever it describes the lamps; nothing else changes, and Rules D 10 to D 28 are untouched.

§ 12 Glossary Wording changed

The entry for the subsidiary placed the lamps beside the head; they stand under it, on the same post.

Subsidiary. Two small white lamps beside the head of some main signals. At the starters WD 4 and WD 7, lit diagonally with the main at Stop they show Shunt: a shunting move may pass at shunting speed as far as the route goes; a train may not. At the homes WD 8 and WD 10 the same lamps are a call-on for a train. Book S, Rules S 10 and S 30.
