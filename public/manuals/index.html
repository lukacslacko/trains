The Library

The Company's manuals, lettered by subject. Each opens as a booklet you may read on screen or print on A5 paper. Every rule the Railway enforces is written in one of these; every control in the cab has its paragraph.

Editions and amendment slips

Every change to a Book raises its edition and is accompanied by an amendment slip: a clean account of what changed, section by section, with the wording before and after. If you know an earlier edition, read the slip and you are current.
